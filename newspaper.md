# The Morning Sunshine

**Motto:** "All the news that's fit to smile about"  
**Edition:** Est. 2026 · Good News Only · Vol. 1  (update with incremental number)
**Published:** Daily at 7 AM  (put exact time of the update)

---

## Editorial Mission

Publish ONLY happy, optimistic, funny, or uplifting news. Every story must leave the reader feeling better than before they read it. All news should be from the date of run

---

## Story Schema

Each edition contains exactly **10 stories**. Every story must include:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `headline` | Catchy headline, max 12 words |
| `deck` | One-sentence journalistic subheadline |
| `body` | 2–3 paragraph newspaper-style article body (~150 words) |
| `category` | One of: Science, Animals, Kindness, Funny, Sports, Community, Environment, Arts, Tech, Health |
| `emoji` | One relevant emoji |
| `imageSearch` | 4–6 word image search query |
| `sourceUrl` | Actual URL of the source article |
| `sourceName` | Publication name |

---

## Categories

Stories must be diverse across categories. Available categories:

- **Science** — discoveries, research breakthroughs, space
- **Funny** — lighthearted, humorous, or absurd good news
- **Sports** — athletic achievements, sportsmanship, records
- **Community** — local heroes, neighbourhood initiatives, civic wins
- **Environment** — nature recovery, clean energy, sustainability milestones
- **Arts** — culture, creativity, music, literature
- **Tech** — positive technology, accessibility tools, helpful innovations
- **Health** — medical breakthroughs, mental wellness, longevity

---

## Layout

The newspaper renders as:

1. **Masthead** — newspaper name, motto, date, edition label
2. **Ticker bar** — single-line edition theme teaser
3. **Featured story** — first story displayed prominently with large emoji and full body text
4. **Section divider** — "More Good News"
5. **Card grid** — remaining 9 stories in a 3-column responsive grid, each showing emoji, category badge, headline, deck, body, and source link
